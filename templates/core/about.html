OUR HERITAGE

Crafting Samosas with Passion & Purpose

Samosey Wala was born from a simple yet ambitious dream: to take India’s quintessential tea-time snack and celebrate its boundless potential. For generations, the samosa has brought people together across streets, family gatherings, and evening chai breaks.

We saw an opportunity to honour this culinary heritage while elevating hygiene, freshness, and creativity. By pairing authentic slow-roasted spice blends with golden, crunchy crusts and playful fillings, Samosey Wala has evolved into a beloved modern snacking destination.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

INGREDIENTS & ETHICS

Our Food Philosophy

We believe good food begins with honest ingredients. Every spice blend is freshly roasted and stone-ground. Our signature crust utilizes a wholesome 50:50 maida and wheat flour blend for an irresistible crunch with lighter indulgence.

UNCOMPROMISED STANDARDS

Quality & Hygiene

Safety and purity are non-negotiable in everything we prepare.

Contactless Cooking

Our kitchens follow strict contactless handling protocols, with regular temperature checks and sanitization.

Controlled Frying Oil

We test oil TPM levels rigorously and replace frying oil daily, guaranteeing crispy taste with zero rancidity.

Eco Food-Grade Boxes

Packed in custom steam-vented, biodegradable boxes to keep your samosas hot and crispy during transit.
